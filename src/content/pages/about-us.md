Art In Motion Dance Center offers competitive and recreational dance training at two Long Island studios, in Melville and Commack. Our focus is technique, so every dancer builds strong fundamentals whatever their goals.

The studio is led by owners and creative directors Siobhan Santapaola and Ali Ribellino. Siobhan is a Long Island native who studied dance at Point Park University and performed professionally, including with the Radio City Rockettes and on Broadway. She has also judged at national competitions.

## Our approach

We train dancers to work hard, stay coachable and support each other. We call that the AIM family.
