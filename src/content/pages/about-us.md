## Mission Statement

At Art In Motion Dance Center, our mission is to provide a quality dance education for competitive and non-competitive students. We focus on all styles of dance while instilling proper technique, creating a positive environment, and giving dancers the tools they need to succeed in the dance and entertainment industry.

## Vision

Our vision is to be a leader in technique-focused dance training on Long Island. AIM is committed to professional, quality instruction while helping develop passionate, dedicated and confident dancers.

## Our Story

Art In Motion Dance Center has served Long Island dancers since 2010. AIM offers state-of-the-art training environments and programs for recreational and competitive dancers at our Melville and Commack locations.

Owner and creative director **Siobhan Santapaola** spent nearly a decade performing professionally in New York City, including as a Radio City Rockette and on the first national tour of *Monty Python's Spamalot*. She has taught master classes, judged national competitions and spent years helping young dancers grow in both technique and confidence.

AIM's faculty brings professional performance, choreography and dance-education experience into the studio while maintaining a supportive, family-focused environment.
