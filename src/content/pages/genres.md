## Ballet
Ballet develops posture, placement, strength, balance and control. It is the technical foundation that supports a dancer across many other styles.

## Jazz
Jazz combines strong technique, musicality and performance quality with energetic movement and theatrical influence.

## Tap
Tap develops rhythm, timing and musicality through precise footwork and percussive sound.

## Lyrical
Lyrical blends ballet and jazz technique with expressive movement, musical interpretation and emotion.

## Contemporary
Contemporary combines elements of modern, jazz, lyrical and classical technique with grounded, expressive movement.

## Hip Hop
Hip Hop draws from street and social dance traditions, developing groove, musicality, rhythm and individual style.

## Acro
Acro combines classical dance technique with floor-based acrobatic skills, strength, flexibility and controlled transitions.

## Pointe
Pointe is an advanced extension of classical ballet technique in which dancers work on fully extended feet in pointe shoes under qualified instruction.
