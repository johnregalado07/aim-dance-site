## Ballet
The foundation of dance technique: posture, strength, balance and control.

## Tap
Rhythm and musicality, played with your feet.

## Contemporary
Expressive, grounded movement that blends technique with storytelling.

## Lyrical
Dance that connects music and emotion, with flowing movement.

## Jazz
High energy, strong technique and performance.

## Hip Hop
Street-inspired movement with rhythm, groove and attitude.
