The AIM Competition Team is designed for dancers who want to take their training and performance experience further.

Company members train in a technique-focused environment, work with AIM faculty and guest artists, rehearse choreography and represent Art In Motion at regional and national events.

## The AIM company experience

- Focused technical training across multiple styles
- Choreography and performance coaching
- Competition and convention opportunities
- Teamwork, accountability and confidence
- A supportive environment built around growth

For audition information, age requirements, rehearsal commitments and current company availability, [contact the studio](/contact).
