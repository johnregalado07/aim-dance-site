Antonia Marchese is a dancer and choreographer from Long Island, New York. She trained competitively in various styles of dance throughout her life, including ballet, tap, jazz, lyrical, contemporary, and hip hop.

She advanced her studies in traditional dance techniques such as modern, ballet, and contemporary at Manhattanville College, where she received a BA in Dance. After college, she continued her dance training and career through continually taking classes with professional dance instructors throughout New York City and teaching in various studios around Westchester and Long Island.

Now, Antonia is the Recreation Supervisor and Fine Arts Director of the Lybrook Recreation Center. The Recreation Center is home to hundreds of yearly classes and events for the community. The Fine Arts Center, founded by Antonia in 2023, sees over 300 students in its dance, acro, and music classes.

Antonia's mission as a teacher is to encourage growth and passion in dance through deep technical training and allowing students to feel like their truest selves when they are on the dance floor.
