Siobhan Santapaola is from Commack NY, she spent almost a decade performing professionally in NYC. Though the love for performing has never left her she thoroughly enjoys passing along her passion. Watching young students grow and thrive.

Siobhan was a Radio City Music Hall Rockette in NYC, other Broadway credits include Monty Python Spamalot (1st National tour). Other favorite performance credits include performing in the Macys Thanksgiving parade twice and as well as Regional Theater credits and Performing for Jean Ann Ryan productions on Silver Sea Cruise line all over South East Asia . Performing on many morning shows and the night show John Oliver.

Teaching credits include directing and owning Art In Motion Dance Center since 2010. As well as the Grand Opening if AIM dance NY in Fort Salonga. She has taught many Master classes at Broadway Dance Center, The Rockette Experience, youth organizations and dance conventions. She has also judged for many dance organizations. Siobhan is so thrilled to be living her passion through dance everyday.
