Siobhan is from Commack, NY and spent nearly a decade performing professionally in New York City. Her credits include the Radio City Rockettes and the first national tour of *Monty Python’s Spamalot*. She has also taught master classes, judged dance competitions and led Art In Motion Dance Center since 2010.
