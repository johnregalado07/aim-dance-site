Natalie Schiff is from Long Island, New York. She began her dance journey at the age of 2 before coming to Art in Motion in 2011. She trained under the Art in Motion staff for 10 years participating in competitions and recitals before coming back to AIM to teach in 2024. Natalie has won a plethora of awards for her dancing in a variety of styles. 

After graduating high school, she continued her education at the University of Virginia studying art and media studies. She has trained in many styles of dance including jazz, tap, lyrical, ballet, contemporary, and hip hop. She continues her dance education through online courses at Dance Teacher Web and attended the Dance Teacher Summit in NYC. Natalie also has trained through Tutu School and teaches at Tutu School Commack! She loves being back teaching with her AIM family!
