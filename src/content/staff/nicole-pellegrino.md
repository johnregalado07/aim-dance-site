Nicole Pellegrino discovered her passion for dance at the age of three and began competing regionally and nationally at ten. Throughout her dance training, she has had the opportunity to study with accomplished artists, including Christina Lynch Markham of the Paul Taylor Dance Company and Ty Lyons Graynor, a former dancer with Graham II and the Limón Dance Company.

She graduated from Hofstra University in December 2024 with a Bachelor of Science degree in Early Childhood and Childhood Education and a minor in Dance. During her time at Hofstra, she had the privilege of working with distinguished dance instructors, including Dyane Harvey-Salaam, Robert Cook, Maiya Redding, Alexandra Williamson, and Jean Benincasa. She went on to earn her Master of Science in Education in Special Education and TESOL in May 2026 and is currently an elementary school teacher.

Since joining the AIM family in 2024, Nicole has enjoyed sharing her love of dance with students of all ages and helping them grow as dancers. She holds certifications in Acrobatic Arts Module I, Dance Teacher Training Level I, and Progressing Ballet Technique Level I. She is passionate about helping her students develop their technique, confidence, and artistry and looks forward to continuing to inspire them in their dance journeys.
