Violetta Gioulis, born and raised in Brooklyn, NY, embarked on her dance journey at a young age. With over 14 years of competitive experience, she is a proud graduate of Art In Motion. Throughout her dancing career, Violetta has performed at notable venues, including the Brooklyn Cyclones and Madison Square Garden. 

In the summer of 2024, she competed for the title of best dancer at The Dance Awards, representing Art In Motion. Most recently in the Summer of 2026, she attended the Joffrey Ballet School Summer Intensive in Catanzaro, Italy, where she continued to refine her technique, artistry and versatility as a dancer.  
Currently, she is pursuing a Bachelors of Arts in Dance at Hofstra University, expanding her love for dance. There, she has had the opportunity to work with acclaimed choreographers including Frederick Earl Mosley, Takehiro Ueyama, and Nicole Von Arx, gaining invaluable insight from each artist. 

Violetta is passionate about creating pieces that dancers will remember forever, work that leaves them feeling seen, connected and proud of the dancer they are. She strives to create choreography that showcases dancers authentically, allowing their individuality to shine through.
