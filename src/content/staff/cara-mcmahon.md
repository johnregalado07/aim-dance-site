Cara McMahon is a young dance professional who was born in Long Island, New York and raised in Cary, North Carolina. She has studied and trained in various dance genres since the age of 2 years old and has been teaching for several years.

Cara attended UNC-Charlotte where she obtained her Bachelor’s Degree in Dance Education, with a certification in grades K-12. While in college, she attended NDA Nationals where she placed 3rd in the nation with her university’s dance team.

Some of her performance credits include The Village People, The Macy’s Parade, Elevate Performance Company, and a restaging of Paul Taylor’s famous work *Esplanade*.

Cara is constantly striving to become a better teacher and dance educator. She continues to train, teach and choreograph all throughout the United States. With her extensive dance knowledge and experience, Cara hopes to inspire the next generation of dancers and help them to achieve their full potential!
