Rachel Overton is from Long Island, NY and she began dancing at the age of two and trained at her local studio, competing at both regional and national levels. She later danced and served as captain on her high school kickline team before attending the University of Connecticut, where she earned her Bachelor of Science in Elementary Education and a Master of Arts in Curriculum and Instruction. While at UConn, she served as Vice President and choreographer of the UConn Dance Company.

Rachel is also a 5th grade teacher on Long Island and is passionate about helping students grow in both confidence and skill. She has been teaching dance since 2020 and has choreographed award-winning routines at the regional and national levels, including entertainment and choreography awards.

At AIM, Rachel focuses on building strong, well-rounded dancers by emphasizing technique, cleanliness, musicality, performance, and teamwork!
