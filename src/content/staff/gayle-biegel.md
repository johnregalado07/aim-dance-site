Gayle Biegel has a Bachelor of Science Degree in Physical Education from Brooklyn College. She has been teaching gymnastics for 38 years. (Recreation and competitive) Gayle was a competitive coach for about 25 years and she trained competitive gymnasts up to Level 10 (some who have gone to nationals and received full scholarships to college). Additionally, she is a beam coach and choreographer. Gayle has been the head of a gymnastics program at Camp Wah-Nee for 15 years.
