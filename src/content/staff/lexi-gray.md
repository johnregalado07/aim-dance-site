Lexi Gray is a New York City based performer and teacher! She grew up in southern Maryland and began dancing at the age of 3 at a competition studio.

She graduated from Marymount Manhattan College in 2025 with a BFA in Dance with a concentration in Jazz and a BA in Psychology. She has worked with notable choreographers throughout her years in college and beyond. She has trained at the Steps On Broadway Summer Study. Lexi was an MVA+ dancer for Velocity Dance Convention, and she trained at The School at Jacob’s Pillow in the musical theatre ensemble. Lexi was also in the 2023 NYCDA Destiny Rising at the Joyce Theatre.

Lexi loves being a dancer and performer but has a true love for teaching! Lexi teaches at Tutu School’s Lenox Hill, Upper East Side, and Tribeca locations. She is so excited to bring her love of teaching and dance to AIM.

Lexi hopes to instill a deep love of dance into all her students and help them grow in new ways each time they step into the classroom! She wants each student to feel valued and know they are all destined for greatness and success!
