Beginning at age 5, dance was the one activity that stuck for Miss Gianna. Training in varying styles, her strengths lie in jazz and lyrical. Gianna graduated from St. Joseph’s University in Patchogue studying general & special education. Currently, she is a certified teacher before teaching dance in the afternoon. During her college experience, she was a 4-year member of the nationally ranking St. Joseph’s Dance Team. 

Working with local choreographers, her team took home top 10 finishes at UDA nationals in Orlando, Florida. Gianna recently trained in Progressing Ballet Technique (PBT) to learn modern methods for advancing technique. She enjoys finding programs to evolve her training for the betterment of her students as well as continuing to dance herself. One goal she has for her dancers is to ignite their passion while pursuing individual progress. Miss Gianna looks forward to creating pieces both her and her dancers enjoy!
