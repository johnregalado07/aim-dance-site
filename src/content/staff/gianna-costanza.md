Gianna Costanza is a Long Island based dance teacher and choreographer. Training in varying styles, her strengths lie in jazz and lyrical. Gianna graduated from St. Joseph’s University in Patchogue, where she studied general & special education. Here, she was a four-year member of the nationally ranking St. Joseph’s Dance Team. Working with local choreographers, her team took home top 10 finishes at UDA nationals in Orlando, Florida. Before getting to the studio each day, Gianna is a second-year kindergarten teacher! 

When not at the studio or in her classroom, Gianna loves to pursue training opportunities for teaching dance. Recently, Gianna attended the DanceOnce Summit in NYC, and became certified as a level one dance teacher through the University of Las Vegas (Dance Teacher Web Expo Program). Gianna is also an accredited level one Acrobatic Arts instructor and has experience using Progressing Ballet Technique (PBT) to learn modern methods for advancing technique. She enjoys finding programs to evolve her training for the betterment of her students as well as continuing to dance herself. One goal she has for her dancers is to ignite their passion while pursuing individual progress. Gianna looks forward to creating pieces that both challenge and excite her dancers.
