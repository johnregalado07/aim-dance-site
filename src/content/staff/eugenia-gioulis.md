Eugenia Gioulis was born and raised in Brooklyn, NY, where she began her dance education. A proud graduate of Art In Motion, she has over 14 years of competitive experience, including performances at notable venues such as Madison Square Garden and Maimonides Park dancing for the Brooklyn Cyclones. 

She has also trained at prestigious institutions, including the Joffrey Ballet School’s Pre-Professional Summer Intensive in 2023. Currently, Eugenia is pursuing a BA in Dance at Hofstra University,where she is performing works choreographed by classmates and faculty members.
