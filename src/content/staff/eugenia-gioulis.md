Eugenia Gioulis was born and raised in Brooklyn, NY, where she began her dance training at the age of three. With over 12 years of commercial dance experience, she has performed at notable venues, including Madison Square Garden and Maimonides Park as a dancer for the Brooklyn Cyclones. She has continued to expand her training at prestigious institutions, including Joffrey Ballet School’s Pre-Professional Summer Intensive in 2023. 

Eugenia is currently pursuing a BFA in Dance at Hofstra University, where she has performed works by choreographers Rachel List, Kashia Kancey, and Takehiro Ueyama, as well as original works choreographed by her peers. Eugenia also serves as president of Teaching Dance as Art, Hofstra University’s dance education club. She is beyond excited to continue to share her passion for movement and performance with the next generation of dancers.
