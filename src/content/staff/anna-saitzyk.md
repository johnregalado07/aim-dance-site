Originally from Long Island, New York, Anna trained in numerous dance styles at Art In Motion Dance Center, where her passion for dance flourished. She competed in various competitions, earning recognition and titles for her performances.

She also trained on the Long Island Boss Kids team and competed as a member of the Stony Brook University Dance Team at UDA Nationals in Orlando, Florida. She has also received Suffolk County and New York State choreography contest awards, demonstrating her ability to express her creative ideas.

Anna is currently pursuing a double major in Business Management and Psychology at Stony Brook University, specializing in Finance and Marketing. With a strong background in dance, she is excited to share her devotion with students at the studio which helped her thrive.
